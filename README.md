# super-store-sales-dashboard
A Super Store Sales Dashboard is an interactive, visual reporting tool that gives a comprehensive overview of retail sales performance across multiple dimensions. It brings together key metrics, charts, and filters to let business users quickly monitor performance, spot trends, and make data-driven decisions.

📊 Super Store Sales Dashboard

A dynamic Sales Analytics Dashboard that visualizes key performance metrics for a retail superstore, including sales, profits, customer segments, regions, and product sub-categories.

📌 Overview

This dashboard provides an interactive way to explore the Super Store sales data through rich visualizations. It helps business users quickly glean insights on performance trends and identify opportunities for improvement.

🚀 Key Highlights:

Total Sales: 1.57M

Total Profit: 175.26K

Total Quantity Sold: 22K

Region-level comparisons

Sub-category and segment breakdowns

Payment mode profit view

Time series sales by year and month

📈 Dashboard Features
🔹 KPI Summary

Displays major business metrics:

📦 Total Sales

💰 Total Profit

📊 Total Quantity Sold

🔹 Sales by Sub-Category

Bar and pie charts to analyze which product sub-categories contribute most to sales (e.g., Phones, Chairs, Binders, Storage, Accessories).

🔹 Profit by Payment Mode

Treemap view showing profit contributions from:

COD

Online

Cards

🔹 Sales by Region

A line chart comparing total sales across regions: West, East, Central, South.

🔹 Sales by Segment

Donut chart showing distribution of sales across customer segments:

Consumer

Corporate

Home Office

🔹 Sales Over Time

Trend line visualizing monthly sales for 2019 and 2020 with clear seasonal patterns.


🗂 Dataset

Your dataset should include at least the following fields:

Column	Type	Description
OrderDate	Date	When the order was placed
Region	String	Sales region
Segment	String	Customer segment
Sub-Category	String	Product sub-category
Sales	Number	Revenue from sale
Profit	Number	Profit from sale
Quantity	Integer	Number of units sold
PaymentMode	String	e.g., COD, Online, Cards.

📝 Notes

✔ Designed for executives, analysts, and sales teams.
